Community News Bulletin

The Local Community Council has announced several new initiatives for the upcoming month. A large tree plantation drive will be organized to encourage environmental awareness among residents. Health awareness camps will also be conducted in different parts of the city where citizens can receive free health check-ups and medical consultations. The council has planned cultural programs and sports competitions to increase participation among young people and strengthen community relationships. Volunteers are invited to register for these events through the Community Event Portal. Participants will receive certificates of appreciation for their contribution to community development activities. Residents are encouraged to stay updated with all upcoming events and announcements through the official portal.
